Zero-Plaintext Storage Policy

Uploaded media is immediately encrypted in chunks using AES-256-GCM. Unencrypted temporary files are permanently wiped from disk. The symmetric key is encrypted using the recipient's RSA-OAEP public key.

Argon2id & RFC 6238 TOTP MFA
